Privacy Policy for AURA Universal Sync

Last Updated: November 2026

AURA Universal Sync ("AURA", "we", "us") provides an AI-powered personal assistant designed to help users track e-commerce deliveries, refunds, and academic records. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you choose to connect your Google account via OAuth, AURA requests read-only access to your Gmail messages using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope.

  • Specific Data Accessed: We read email sender headers, subjects, and snippets strictly to detect e-commerce shipping notifications, courier tracking numbers, and delivery updates (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, Purplle, Mamaearth).
  • Data Not Accessed: We do not read, process, or store personal conversations, confidential correspondence, or emails unrelated to orders.

2. How We Use Google User Data

We use your Gmail data solely to provide customer-facing features within the AURA assistant interface—specifically to parse tracking numbers, delivery partners, and order statuses.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

AURA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, unless necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger/acquisition.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train, retrain, or fine-tune general AI, foundation, or machine learning models.
  • Human interaction with user email data is strictly prohibited unless authorized by the user for technical troubleshooting or security investigation.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Parsed order summaries and transient tokens are stored securely in session storage and an encrypted local SQLite database. You can permanently revoke access and disconnect your Google account at any time by clicking the Disconnect button in the application sidebar, which purges the stored session tokens.
