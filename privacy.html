Privacy Policy for AURA Universal Sync

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

AURA Universal Sync is an intelligent personal AI assistant that assists users with tracking e-commerce orders, banking and UPI refund statuses, and academic records.

2. Google User Data Access & Permissions

AURA Universal Sync requests permission to access the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope. This access is strictly utilized to scan and retrieve transactional delivery confirmations and order status receipts from known merchant senders upon user request.

3. Data Usage, Sharing & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or transfer personal Google user data to any external parties or third-party advertising brokers. All data processed is used solely to provide user-facing capabilities in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Revoking Access

Users may disconnect their connected Gmail account at any time using the "Disconnect" button in the application interface, or by revoking permissions directly through their Google Account security management page.
